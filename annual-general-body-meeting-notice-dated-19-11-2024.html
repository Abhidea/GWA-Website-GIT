ANNUAL GENERAL BODY MEETING NOTICE (Dec 29, 2024) 

The 37th Annual General Body Meeting of Girinagar Welfare Association, Bengaluru-85 will be held on Sunday, 29th Dec 2024, 10.00 AM at “Sri Srinivasa Nilaya Dharmika Sabha Mandir (opposite Vivekananda Park), Girinagar, Bengaluru-560085.
All the Members of the Association are requested to attend the meeting. Balance Sheet and Audited Accounts of the Association for the year 2023-24 will be issued separately. However, those who are interested to peruse the same may visit Association office between 6.30 PM-8.00 PM from 23-12-2024 to 28-12-2024.

Programme for Annual General Body Meeting:

  1. Invocation.
  2. Welcome Address by the President.
  3. Observing silence in memory of departed souls of Members.
  4. Presentation of Annual Report, Audited Accounts and Audit Report for the year 2023-24 followed by discussion on the same, if any.
  5. Appointment of Auditor’s for the year 2025-26.
  6. Distribution of “Pratibha Puraskara” by Sri.L.A.Ravi Subramanya. M.L.A
  7. Address by Sri. L.A. Ravi Subramanya, M.L.A.
  8. Any Other matter for Discussion.
  9. Vote of Thanks.

Note: Buffet Lunch is being arranged at 1.00 PM.

Sd/- S.VASUDEV, President
