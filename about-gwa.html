About GWA

Vision

Unite the community of residents of Girinagar with harmony and cooperation and ensure success of community initiatives.

Mission

Committing, contributing, and marching forward with guidance of elders and active participation of youngsters to sustain a happy community of residents.

Values

Civic sense in maintaining cleanliness, following regulations, avoiding wastage of resources. Inspiring kids and guiding youngsters to be responsible community members. A cordial and enriching environment for families.

Our History

1986

Girinagar Welfare Association (GWA) was registered on Nov, 1 as a Non Profit non-Governmental body under Karnataka Societies Act 1960.

1990

Construction of overhead tank was completed to store water pumped from baseline tank at Netkalappa circle. Layout started getting piped water.

1992

GWA collected signature campaign & pressured BWSSB to complete Cauvery Stage 3 laying of main line to Girinagar Layout. It was completed by June 1992.

1998

The efforts of GWA and representation from the members of the Society compelled BDA to pass a resolution to issue No objection Certificate to the BBMP to issue Khatas to the site holders in II & III Phases of the layout by collecting betterment charges and taxes from 1995. As it was a fully developed layout with all the civic facilities the charges related to development of civic amenity areas of the layout were excluded.

2010 & 2011

BDA declared the II & III Phase layouts as unauthorized layouts by passing resolutions and stopped issue of Khatas and sanctioning of building plans.

2014

GWA approached High Court of Karnataka against the unjust resolutions passed by the BDA in the years 2010 & 2011. High Court quashed the resolutions passed by the BDA as unjust, not maintainable and without legal justification and gave directions to BBMP to transfer the Khatas and sanction building plans in respect of the Girinagar II & III Phase layouts.

2016

GWA celebrated its 30th Anniversary on Dec 25 and a Souvenir was published on the occasion. Twenty past office bearers of the Association who tirelessly served and brought Cauvery water to the layout and the most important Resolution No.108/98 of BDA to enable BBMP to issue Khatas for the sites in II and III phase layouts were honoured on the occasion.
