Award Scheme for Merit Students of CBSE, ICSE, PUC and S.S.L.C for the year 2023-2024

Award Scheme for Merit Students of CBSE, ICSE, PUC and S.S.L.C for the year 2023-2024 will be awarded under the following conditions.
(a)  Student should be a normal Resident of Girinagar.
(b)  Parents/Grand Parents of the Student should be a Life Member of the Association.
(c)  Student should have scored more than 60% of marks in the Examination conducted during the academic year of April/May 2024 within Bangalore City area.
(d) Students should Produce Photocopy of final marks card along with a covering letter for consideration on or before 20th December 2024 to the Secretary, Address: No.693, Ground Floor, 1st “C” Main , 11th Cross, Girinagar 2nd Phase, Bengaluru-560085 (Ph.No.26722903 / Mob.No.9845103463) or any of the Executive Committee Members between 6.30-8.00pm.
A Sub-Committee under the Chairmanship of the President has been formed to process the application and the decision of the Sub-Committee is final and binding and no appeal will be entertained.  The Committee will Award 3 Prizes in each Category as 1st, 2nd and 3rd Prize.
